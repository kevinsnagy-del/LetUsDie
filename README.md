# Let Us Die *(beta)*

Online co-op for **LET IT DIE** through Steam, built on [Mod Drive 9000](https://github.com/kevinsnagy-del/ModDrive9000). It works in the base game, and in POSTMORTEM games when the [POSTMORTEM](https://github.com/kevinsnagy-del/Postmortem) plug-in is installed.

**Every player needs Mod Drive 9000 and Let Us Die, on the same versions.**

This is a **beta**: co-op is new ground in this game, so expect desyncs and rough edges. Your saves are backed up before every install (`ModDriveInstaller\ModDrive9000.exe`, **Backups**).

## Features

### Lobbies
- A **MULTIPLAYER** button in the pause menu: host a room or join one.
- **A room list:** open rooms are listed, so you can join without an invite.
- **Room view:** see who is in the room; the host can kick (two steps), and anyone can leave or close the room.
- **Same-mod check:** the game files of every player are compared before you play, so a version mismatch is caught at the start.

### Playing together
- **See each other:** every player's character wears their own gear, moves and animates (walking, guarding, dodging, jumping, attacking), dies and comes back.
- **Follow the host:** the host moves the party between floors; a joining player gets the elevator stops they need.
- **The same floor:** followers play the host's floor layout, the same enemies and the same Pale Wind mushrooms.
- **Enemies in sync:** the host decides enemy health, position and death; enemies go after every player; status ailments are shared.
- **The world in sync:** boxes, breakables and ambush lockdowns.
- **Shared lives:** the death screen shares the party's lives instead of charging kill coins.
- **Friendly fire:** off by default, switchable for the room.
- **Ready check** before floor changes and at boss doors.
- **Pings:** mark a spot or an enemy for the whole party.
- **Co-op notices**, and co-op error codes on the game's own error screen.
- **POSTMORTEM runs** can be played together when POSTMORTEM is installed.

### Options
Under **Options, then Mod Drive 9000, then Let Us Die**:
- **Party panel:** game style, classic or off; its size (30-100 % of your own health plate); its position (below your health plate, the four corners, middle left or right) and X / Y offsets; and a preview with three sample members.
- **Health bars over allies:** the game's own name plate (name, level, HP) over each party member.
- **Waiting-room visitors:** party members see each other in the waiting room.

## Install

1. Install [Mod Drive 9000](https://github.com/kevinsnagy-del/ModDrive9000) first.
2. Download `LetUsDie-<version>.zip` from [Releases](../../releases), close the game, and unzip it into the LET IT DIE folder.
3. Open `ModDriveInstaller\ModDrive9000.exe` and choose **Apply mods to the game**.

**Updates and removal:** in `ModDriveInstaller\ModDrive9000.exe`, under **Mods**.
